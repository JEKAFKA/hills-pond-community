About Our Lake

Hills Pond is an 18-acre lake in the northeastern corner of Washington County, just west of Crystal Lake and about 6.8 miles from the village of Whitehall. Gas motors are not permitted on the water, which keeps the lake calm and quiet for swimming, paddling, fishing, and wildlife year-round. Hills Pond Road follows the shoreline and ties the community together.

This site is a simple hub for the people who live around and love the lake. Have something to add or correct? Reach out through the community group.
